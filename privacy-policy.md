## Privacy Policy for Down Beyond

**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026

Down Beyond ("the Extension") is a client-side browser extension designed to help users export their TTRPG data. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to data privacy and security.

### 1. No Data Collection or Processing

The developer of this Extension does not collect, store, track, or monitor any personal data, browsing history, or user credentials.
• All operations are performed entirely client-side within your browser instance.
• The Extension converts character data directly into Markdown text inside your browser.
• We do not operate external databases, tracking analytics, or backend servers to store or receive your data.

### 2. Local Storage and Data Scope

Any data read by the extension remains local to your device. The Extension uses local browser capabilities to render the layout and handle your text exports. No information is transmitted across the network by this Extension.
